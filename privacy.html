Privacy Policy

PlanThenRoam Seller Tools

Last updated: 5 September 2026

PlanThenRoam Seller Tools is a private seller-management application used by the owner of PlanThenRoam to manage and schedule content for the owner’s own online shop and social-media accounts.

Information the application may process

When platform integrations are connected, the application may process information needed to perform user-requested actions, such as account identifiers, board information, Pin content and metadata, listing identifiers, listing URLs, titles, descriptions, tags, uploaded media, scheduling information, and content-performance data where access has been authorised.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the application’s seller-management functions, including preparing and scheduling social-media content, matching content to the correct destination or product, managing the owner’s own listings, and improving the owner’s content workflow.

User control

The application is intended for the owner’s personal business use. Content is selected and approved by the user before it is queued or published. The application does not publish third-party content on behalf of unrelated users.

Storage and security

Non-sensitive workflow data may be stored in the user’s browser or in services used to operate the application. API credentials and access tokens are not intentionally stored in publicly accessible webpage code. Where platform credentials are required, they will be stored using appropriate private or secret-storage mechanisms.

Sharing and sale of data

PlanThenRoam Seller Tools does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with advertisers. Data may be transmitted to the relevant connected platform or technical service only when necessary to perform a requested application function.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application’s functions or to maintain the owner’s saved workflow. Local application data can be removed by clearing the application’s stored data. Connected-platform access can be revoked through the relevant platform account settings.

Third-party platforms

The application may connect to Pinterest and Etsy. Use of those services is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be raised through the PlanThenRoam Etsy shop.
